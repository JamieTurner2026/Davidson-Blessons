# Selah audit — 2026-10-09

**Question asked:** Do the games make sense? Are they neither too easy nor too hard? Do they ask
correct questions about the passage? Does the app hang together, and how do we simplify it?

**Method:**
- Three parallel code reviews: question generation and difficulty, page structure, and the reward economy.
- A live walk-through of production (home page, John 3 lesson).
- Every critical finding re-checked in source.
- Two scripts that run the real code against live Bible text:
  - `scripts/check-rounds.ts` generates every lesson and challenge round for 32 chapters in BSB,
    NIV and KJV, 12 attempts each: 11,076 rounds.
  - `scripts/verify-trivia.ts` checks the curated bank and the daily-quiz pool against BSB.

## Verdict

| Area | Before | After this session |
|---|---|---|
| Correctness of generated questions | 424 distinct broken or unfair round patterns | **0** (same 11,076-round sweep) |
| Difficulty | Too easy: 3 options, the answer often guessable from its format | 4 options in 97% of rounds; format no longer gives the answer away |
| Curated questions | 0 verifier failures, but 6 items with a second defensible answer and several giveaway distractors | 15 items fixed; stricter verifier still at 0 failures |
| Daily "Which book?" | 3 verses named their own book, 5 had a twin, and the options gave away the answer | 10 verses swapped; options drawn from the same section of the Bible |
| Saved data | One bad field silently wiped everything | Backup, then field-by-field salvage |
| Rewards | XP farmable without reading; one chapter couldn't meet the daily goal | Rewards follow reading; one chapter lights the lamp |
| Site structure | 12 routes, 10 nav links, about 30 calls to action on home | Unchanged. This is the next phase (proposal below) |

## Critical findings (all fixed)

1. **Saved data could be wiped permanently.** If any one stored field failed validation, the whole
   state reset to empty, and the next save overwrote the user's real data
   (`lib/study-store.ts` `readStorage`). One way to trigger it: memorizing a passage longer than 2,000
   characters. **Fix:** the raw value is copied to `selah:v1:backup:<day>`, then every field and
   record entry that still parses is kept. Checked in the browser with an injected bad card: the note,
   XP and good card survived.
2. **Fill-in-the-blank broke on em dashes.** "morning—the" became the fake word "morningthe", which
   was chosen as the answer. The clue then showed the whole verse with no blank (Gen 1:31, Exod 20:10,
   and 15 more patterns). Repeated words ("created" ×3 in Gen 1:27) also left the answer visible.
   **Fix:** split on dashes, hide only words that appear once, replace by position, and refuse any clue
   without a blank.
3. **The reader joined text without spaces** ("lifeSo"), which fed broken text into the
   post-chapter challenge and memory cards. **Fix:** a shared `appendRun()`.
4. **The daily quiz gave answers away.**
   - Ruth 1:16, Ezra 7:10 and 1 Sam 16:7 name their own book.
   - These had a near-identical twin: Exod 20:3 = Deut 5:7, 1 Chr 16:34 ≈ Psalms,
     2 Sam 22:2 ≈ Ps 18:2, Mark 10:45 ≈ Matt 20:28, Mark 12:30 ≈ Matt 22:37.
   - Live on 2026-10-09: Habakkuk was the only prophet among Genesis, 2 Samuel and 1 Kings.

## High findings (all fixed)

- **XP could be farmed without reading.**
  - Chapter XP had no daily cap.
  - A lesson marked its chapter read with no reading check.
  - Plan checkboxes paid 15 XP each and could be unticked and re-ticked.
- **The daily goal (20 XP) couldn't be met by one chapter (10 XP),** while the home page said
  "Read a chapter to light it."
- **Curated questions with a second correct answer:**
  - "Speak, for Your servant is listening": Eli said it first (1 Sam 3:9)
  - Ur vs. Haran
  - "Daniel and Hananiah": Hananiah *is* Shadrach
  - Hannah vs. Anna
  - The Lydia question cited a verse that names Thyatira, not Philippi
- **Checkpoints asked about other books.** A Genesis 1–5 checkpoint could ask about Joseph or Exodus.
- **Lightning rewarded mashing.** Each card is a 50/50 and wrong answers cost nothing.
- **Rounds were easy to guess:**
  - The "match the verse number" round could be solved by story order.
  - "…" appeared on some options but not others.
  - A lone capitalized option among lowercase ones.

## Is it too easy or too hard now?

- **Lessons:** medium.
  - Every round comes from the passage just read.
  - It has 4 options, and the format no longer gives the answer away.
  - Wrong options come from nearby verses (e.g. "What comes after verse 5?" offers verse 4 as a trap).
  - There's a short reading pause before play, about 400 words a minute, 4–20 s.
- **Curated bank:** still about 70% easy (ark, Goliath, "Who baptized Jesus?"). Fine for a first
  stop, too easy for regular players. The fix is the difficulty tags below.
- **Lightning:** fair now. Score = right − wrong, so guessing scores about zero.
- **No fail state, no lives:** by design ("encourage, never punish"). Stars set the challenge:
  2★ at 60%, 3★ at 90%.

## Recommendations — next phase

### 1. Simplify to four destinations
| Destination | What it holds | Replaces |
|---|---|---|
| **Today** (`/`) | One main button: "Today's stop: John 3 →" (opens the lesson). A small row: streak · goal · verses due. The trail. | Home's 4-button row, 8 section cards, "Your journey" card, separate daily quiz |
| **Bible** | Reader, plus a Books view with a Trail/Shelf toggle. Search in the reader toolbar. Plans choose what "today's stop" is and tick themselves. | `/read`, `/map`, `/library`, `/plans`, `/search` |
| **Practice** | Your verses (spaced review) first, then the Daily mix and games. Checkpoints stay on the trail. | `/play`, `/memory` |
| **Me** | Progress, badges, highlights and notes (with backup), Workshop as an optional extra | `/journey`, `/notes`, `/workshop` |

This takes the nav from 10 links to 4 and home from about 30 things to click to one main button. It
also brings 7 "where am I in the Bible?" views down to 1. "Journey" will mean one thing only: a book
on the trail.

### 2. Trim the rewards from about 20 numbers to 6
**Keep:** level (with the pilgrim as its picture), streak with rest days, stars, badges (reading and
memory milestones), today's goal, verses due.

**Merge or cut:**
- Oil becomes "spend stars". Drop the XP÷5 conversion.
- Drop reading combos (they reward speed, which fights the pause that "Selah" means).
- Drop both treasure hunts, the Collector badge and per-game best scores.

### 3. Make the game better
- Add a `difficulty` tag (1–3) to every bank item. Pick difficulty from level plus recent accuracy:
  two 3★ results in a row raise it, a 1★ lowers it.
- Rebalance the bank toward medium: add about 80 "why/how" questions for the 30 most-read chapters.
- Add comprehension rounds ("Why did Nicodemus come at night?") for popular chapters.
- Bring back missed questions a few days later, the same way as memory review.
- End each lesson with one ungraded "Selah" prompt: a question to sit with, no score.
- Raise the round count for very long chapters (Psalm 119).

## How to re-run the checks
```bash
npx tsx scripts/check-rounds.ts     # every generated round, 32 chapters × BSB/NIV/KJV
npx tsx scripts/verify-trivia.ts    # curated bank + daily-quiz pool against BSB
```
